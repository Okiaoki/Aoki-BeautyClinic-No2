"The essence of beauty lies in harmony."

True beauty is found in harmony.

At Aoki Beauty Clinic, we pursue not a uniform ideal of beauty, but the beauty that is uniquely yours, attuned to each person's bone structure, skin, and lifestyle. Physicians who combine the proven skill of plastic surgery with the refined sensibility of aesthetic medicine personally oversee every stage, from consultation and treatment through to aftercare.

Director Seiichi Aoki

DIRECTOR

Seiichi Aoki

Aoki Seiichi

Seiichi Aoki, M.D.

After 10 years refining his craft in reconstructive and cosmetic surgery within a university hospital's plastic surgery department, he went on to perform more than 2,000 procedures a year at a leading aesthetic clinic. Specializing in rhinoplasty and eye surgery, he has now completed over 15,000 procedures in total.

"In cosmetic surgery, decisions of a single millimeter determine the final result. I am always seeking to balance anatomical precision with a beauty that feels natural for each individual."

In 2020 he founded Aoki Beauty Clinic. By uniting the safety of a plastic-surgery foundation with attentive counseling that honors each patient's ideals, the clinic sustains a repeat rate of over 85%.

VICE DIRECTOR

Misaki Shiraishi

Shiraishi Misaki

Misaki Shiraishi, M.D.

After serving as a board-certified dermatologist at a university hospital, she turned to aesthetic dermatology. Focusing on laser and injectable treatments, she has performed more than 8,000 procedures.

"The skin reflects a person's very way of life. My goal is not merely to erase spots and wrinkles, but to elevate the overall quality of the skin so that you can feel confident in your bare complexion."

In injectable treatments she holds to an "aesthetic of restraint," earning a reputation for natural-looking results. While actively embracing the latest advances in aesthetic medicine, she offers only safe, evidence-based treatments.
