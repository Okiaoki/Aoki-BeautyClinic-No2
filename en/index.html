About Us

About Aoki Beauty Clinic

Just a 3-minute walk from Omotesando Station. Aoki Beauty Clinic is a comprehensive aesthetic clinic offering both cosmetic surgery and dermatology services.

Director Aoki brings over 10 years of experience from leading aesthetic clinic groups, performing more than 2,000 procedures annually. Founded in 2021 on the principles of "proven expertise and sincere dialogue," our clinic carefully evaluates each patient's bone structure, skin type, and lifestyle to recommend only the treatments truly needed — delivering fully personalized aesthetic care.

In the 4 years since opening, we have amassed over 10,000 case results. We remain committed to delivering outcomes that leave every patient glad they chose us.

Why Choose Us

Why Choose Aoki Beauty Clinic

01

Personalized
Treatment Plans

We take the time to thoroughly understand each patient's bone structure, skin type, and lifestyle, then design a treatment plan that brings you as close as possible to your ideal self. Rather than one-size-fits-all procedures, we prioritize planning tailored exclusively to you.

02

Advanced Equipment &
Proven Expertise

We continuously adopt the latest evidence-based medical devices and techniques. Our director performs over 2,000 procedures per year, delivering high-precision results from delicate eyelid surgery to cutting-edge laser treatments.

03

Comprehensive Aftercare

We maintain a thorough follow-up system after every procedure. Beyond routine post-operative monitoring, we provide an environment where you can consult with us anytime about any concerns. All follow-up visits are complimentary.

For International Patients

Welcome to Aoki Beauty Clinic. We are pleased to offer consultations and treatments to international patients visiting Tokyo.

What We Offer

  • Consultations available in English (and Mandarin Chinese)
  • Detailed treatment plans provided before your visit
  • Coordination with your travel schedule for optimal treatment timing
  • Post-treatment follow-up via email or video call

Before Your Visit

  1. Submit an online consultation request with photos
  2. Receive a personalized treatment plan and cost estimate
  3. Book your appointment at a time that suits your schedule
  4. Visit our clinic in Omotesando, Tokyo

Payment
