About Us

About Aoki Beauty Clinic

A 3-minute walk from Omotesando Station, Aoki Beauty Clinic is a comprehensive aesthetic clinic combining cosmetic surgery and aesthetic dermatology.

Director Aoki spent more than ten years at a major aesthetic clinic group, performing over 2,000 procedures each year. Guided by a philosophy of "refined technique and honest dialogue," he opened the clinic in 2021. By carefully assessing each patient's bone structure, skin quality and lifestyle, we practice bespoke aesthetic medicine that recommends only the treatments truly needed.

In the 4 years since opening, we have built a track record of more than 10,000 cases. We remain committed to delivering results that leave every patient genuinely glad they chose us.

Concept

Beauty, with certainty. The essence of beauty lies in harmony.

What we strive for is a beauty true to each person, unswayed by passing trends. By reading your bone structure, skin quality and lifestyle—and embracing an aesthetic of subtraction rather than mere addition—we pursue results that are natural and refined.

Refined technique and honest dialogue. From consultation through aftercare, we stay by your side at every stage, delivering results you will be genuinely glad you chose.

Why Choose Us

Why Patients Choose Aoki Beauty Clinic

01

Bespoke Treatment
Tailored to Each Individual

We take the time to understand each patient's bone structure, skin quality and lifestyle, then design a treatment plan that brings you as close as possible to the self you wish to become. Rather than one-size-fits-all procedures, we value planning made for you alone.

02

Advanced Equipment
and Proven Technique

We adopt medical equipment and techniques grounded in the latest evidence. With experience of more than 2,000 procedures a year, the director performs everything from delicate eyelid surgery to the latest laser treatments with exacting precision.

03

Reassuring Aftercare

We maintain a thorough follow-up system after every procedure. Beyond post-treatment monitoring, we provide an environment where you can consult us at any time about any worry or concern. We never charge a follow-up consultation fee.
